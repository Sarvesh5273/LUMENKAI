## science-academies-srfp

URL read: https://webjapps.ias.ac.in/fellowship2026/index.html

- Student status and location: “The three national Science Academies offer several two-month Summer Fellowships to enable students/teachers (studying/teaching in India) to work with scientists associated with the three Academies during 2026.”
- Eligible institutions: “Applications are invited from motivated students and teachers from all universities and colleges affiliated to UGC/AICTE/MCI/Accredited Institutions/Private Universities for these Fellowships.”
- Deadline: “The last date for receipt of applications online is 31 January 2026.”
- Funding: “The selected students/teachers will be provided with entitled round trip train fare and a monthly fellowship to meet their living expenses at the place of work under the Fellowship program.”

Doubt: The announcement says detailed eligibility criteria are displayed in the online application, but the closed application no longer exposes them. Any percentage or CGPA cutoff needs checking in the next application form. This is why the record is `needs_check`.

## iusstf-viterbi

URL read: https://iusstf.org/iusstf-viterbi-program

- Citizenship and student status: “Indian students currently pursuing a Bachelors or Master’s degree at recognized institutions of higher education in India.”
- Branches: “Open to students of Electrical Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Computer Engineering and Computational Sciences.”
- GPA: “Grade Point Average (GPA) of 8.5 or higher (or equivalent of 3.5 GPA on a 4.0 scale); Percentage equivalent: 80% and higher.”
- Cohort: “Students must be in their 3rd year of a 4-year Bachelor's program, 3rd or 4th year of a 5-year Bachelor's program, or the first year of a Master's program. The degree completion date must be on or after May 2027.”
- Deadline: “Submission Deadline Extended: December 31, 2025, at 12:00 PM (Noon) IST.”
- Funding: “Stipend (to cover contingencies including Network access, equipment, computing facilities etc)”, “Airfare” and “Accommodation”.

Doubt: None affecting the structured rules. The 8.5 value is entered directly. The separately stated 3.5/4.0 and 80 percent equivalents were not converted.

## Programs researched and excluded

- **Mitacs Globalink Research Internship:** The current official 2027 program page lists eligible applicant countries and does not include India. Excluded because the record would incorrectly tell Indian students they can apply.
- **DAAD WISE:** Current WISE eligibility and deadline could not be located on a readable official DAAD page. Search results surfaced other DAAD programs and unofficial summaries, which were not used.
- **Amazon ML Summer School India:** No program criteria page on an Amazon-owned domain or an official registration page demonstrably linked by Amazon was found. It is also a training school rather than a funded internship.
- **Adobe India Women-in-Technology Scholarship:** Adobe’s current research programs page does not list this scholarship. The official Adobe material found was an old winners announcement, not a current application page.
- **Microsoft Research India internships:** The official lab opportunities page currently lists staff roles but no student internship call with eligibility and a deadline.
