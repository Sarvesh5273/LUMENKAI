## smart-india-hackathon

- Source read: https://www.sih.gov.in/letters/2026/SIH%202026%20Guidelines.pdf
- Student status and route: “Students: You cannot register directly. Your college’s SPOC (Single Point of Contact) will handle registrations.” The criteria section adds, “Only students selected in the Internal Hackathon can be registered.”
- Team rules: “All team members must be from the same college (no inter-college teams)”; “Each team consists of 6 members, including the team leader”; and “At least one female team member is mandatory.” The female-member rule applies to the team, not to every applicant, so gender is `any`.
- Deadline: “The last date for team nomination and idea submission by College SPOC and Team leader on SIH portal is till 30th Sept 2026 only.”
- The published student registration and team-formation criteria include no marks, CGPA, backlog, gap-year, branch, graduation-year, citizenship or work-experience cutoff.
- Doubt: none for the encoded rules. College internal hackathons determine which teams the SPOC may nominate.

## revenuecat-shipaton

- Source read: https://revenuecat-shipaton-2026.devpost.com/rules
- Deadline: “Submission Period: Friday, July 31, 2026 at 8:00am PDT – Wednesday, September 30, 2026 at 11:45pm PDT.”
- Main eligibility: “Individuals who are at least the age of majority where they reside as of the time of entry”; “Teams of Eligible Individuals”; and eligible organisations may enter.
- Minor category: “Individuals who are under the age of majority where they reside but are at least thirteen (13) years of age as of the time of entry (‘Minor Entrants’), solely for purposes of entering and winning the Next Gen Award.”

- The official eligibility section has no academic marks, CGPA, backlog, gap-year, degree, graduation-year, branch or work-experience rule.
- Doubt: none for the encoded main-competition rules.

## reliance-foundation-undergraduate-scholarship

- Sources read: https://scholarships.reliancefoundation.org/UG_Scholarship.aspx and https://scholarships.reliancefoundation.org/assets/pdf/UG_FAQ.pdf
- Deadline: “The deadline to submit your application is 11:59 PM IST on Monday, 5 October 2026.”
- Citizenship: “Be an Indian citizen residing in India.”
- Class 12 cutoff: “Have passed Class 12 with a minimum of 60% marks.”
- Student and cohort rule: “Be enrolled in the first year (academic year 2026–27) of a regular, full-time undergraduate” degree. First-year BTech in 2026-27 is translated to graduation year 2030.
- Income rule retained in notes: “Have a household income of ₹15 lakh or less (preference will be given to applicants with a household income below ₹2.5 lakh).”
- Stream: the program page says first-year students may be “pursuing any subject stream,” so branches are `any`.
- Gender: the page says it aims to encourage applications by girls, not that it is women-only, so gender is `any`.
- No Class 10, CGPA, backlog, gap-year or work-experience threshold appears in the official eligibility list.
- Doubt: none for the encoded rules.
