# Startup programs verification log

Rules for this category:

- Money must be a grant, a fellowship stipend or equity-free funding a student can actually receive. Cloud credits, tool discounts and "perks" alone do not qualify.
- Government portals are out of scope for this app.
- `amount_status: 'stated'` only with the figure copied from the official page.

## Reviewed and dropped

- YC Summer Grants: the 2026 page (https://events.ycombinator.com/summer-grants-26) stated "$20,000 in cash" and "rolling applications", but the event data closed applications on 31 July 2026 and the programme ended on 21 August 2026, and no earlier edition exists. Dropped on 2026-09-22 under the recurring-programs rule; add it back only if a 2027 page appears.
- Cartier Women's Initiative: verified from https://www.cartierwomensinitiative.com/regional-awards ($100,000, $60,000 and $30,000 grants; women founders; deadline June 16, 2026 for the 2027 edition), but dropped because “The business should have generated annual revenue between $50,000 USD and $5 million USD in its last closed fiscal year” and must employ 5 to 250 people. Those conditions cannot be encoded as rules, so a student would see the door as open when it is not.
- Hult Prize: "one of our top 8 finalists will secure $1M in seed investment to advance their business. The EF Hult Prize Foundation receives an equity stake in the winning startup (typically 10%)." This is an equity investment, not a prize, grant or stipend.
- Hello Tomorrow Global Challenge: the current official page describes the cash as a "€200K equity prize", so it is an equity investment rather than an eligible grant or prize.
- Campus Fund: the official page states student-founder eligibility but does not publish what founders receive.
- 1517 Medici Project: the official page says it cannot issue grants to teams based outside North America.
- Awesome Foundation: the official chapter list has no India chapter, and no current India route could be verified.
- Z Fellows: the official page now offers an optional $10,000 equity investment, not a grant, prize or fellowship stipend.
- Y Combinator standard program: its official $500,000 standard deal is an equity investment, not a grant, prize or fellowship stipend supported by this bucket.
- Techstars: official pages state the investment terms but do not state that students can apply.
- Ethereum Foundation Ecosystem Support Program: the current official applicant pages do not publish a traceable amount for an individual open application.
- Entrepreneur First: no official page was found that states money paid to founders before investment for an India-accessible program.
- Red Bull Basement: no official page was found stating a cash prize.
