## Key Facts
- **Google route is not realistically available from a fresh personal account by Sept. 30.** Rule fact: production access requires 12 testers continuously opted in for 14 days, followed by an application that Google says typically takes ≤7 days but may take longer and is not guaranteed. From Sept. 21, even the testing minimum alone ends after the deadline. URL: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en-IN
- Google onboarding also has a **US$25 one-time fee and identity verification**. URL: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435?hl=en
- **Apple is possible only in the narrow sense that no mandatory 14-day gate was found.** Enrollment requires supported Apple hardware, 2FA, iCloud, government ID, and an annual nonrefundable membership; Apple states no fixed enrollment timeline here, and incorrect identity data can delay review. This is not a promise of enrollment or App Store approval within ten days. URL: https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/enrolling-in-the-app
- **Samsung is also high-risk from zero accounts.** Its official guide requires Samsung/Seller Portal setup and commercial seller approval; approval typically takes several days, while bank or D‑U‑N‑S verification can take up to 10 business days. Approval is not guaranteed. URL: https://developer.samsung.com/galaxy-store/prepare.html
- Shipaton’s official announcement says the app must be brand-new, first shipped to an eligible real store by Sept. 30, and use RevenueCat for at least one IAP or RevenueCat Ads. URL: https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/company/announcing-shipaton-2026
- Search-result evidence for the official rules gives a submission cutoff of **Sept. 30, 2026, 11:45 p.m. PDT**. URL: https://revenuecat-shipaton-2026.devpost.com/rules

**Skeptical conclusion:** A fresh Google personal account cannot qualify through a first Google Play production release by the deadline without evasion. Apple is the only remotely plausible route found, but requires immediate enrollment, compatible Apple hardware, monetization setup, build completion, and uncertain reviews. Samsung’s verification ranges consume most or all of the remaining window. Spending ten days is therefore a speculative bet, not a reasonable expectation of prize eligibility.
