# Next Gen Award

A new Shipaton prize category dedicated to students building mobile apps, with no store submission or paid developer account required.

## A lower-friction path into Shipaton.

The Next Gen Award is the student category for Shipaton 2026: a place for students to build, learn, and show what they can make by submitting a video and source code instead of an app store listing.

### Built for students

The Next Gen Award gives student builders their own prize lane inside Shipaton, whether they are in high school, university, bootcamp, or a campus dev club.

### No store submission required

Students can submit a video and source code, so they do not need to buy an Apple or Google developer account just to participate.

### Still in the prize mix

Next Gen Award projects will also be part of the broader Shipaton prize lineup. The famous Shipaton prize announcements are coming soon.

## Who is eligible?

The Next Gen Award is for students in high school, college, university, bootcamp, or another academic program. To submit, you will need to use a student or academic email on Devpost. Check your email here first, then continue if it looks good. Domain matching is based on JetBrains/swot.
